Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

Effective date: February 23, 2026

Introduction

AlphaGrab ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our sports ticket marketplace website and services.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, phone number
  • Payment Information: Credit/debit card details, billing address (processed securely by payment providers)
  • Profile Information: Preferences, ticket history, favorite teams/events
  • Communication Data: Customer support interactions, feedback

Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, device identifiers
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location (with consent)
  • Cookies and Tracking: As described in our Cookie Policy

How We Use Your Information

Service Provision

  • Process ticket purchases and reservations
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide customer support
  • Send transaction confirmations and updates

Personalization and Analytics

  • Customize content based on preferences
  • Recommend relevant events and tickets
  • Analyze website usage and performance
  • Improve user experience and features

Marketing and Communications

  • Send promotional offers (with consent)
  • Personalize advertisements (with consent)
  • Conduct market research and analysis

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Union, we process personal data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity: To fulfill our contractual obligations for ticket services
  • Legitimate Interests: To prevent fraud, ensure security, and improve our services
  • Consent: For marketing, personalization, and non-essential cookies
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

  • Payment Processors: Stripe, PayPal for secure payment processing
  • Ticket Vendors: Official ticket providers and venues
  • Analytics Providers: Google Analytics (with consent)
  • Marketing Platforms: Social media and advertising platforms (with consent)

Legal Requirements

  • To comply with legal obligations and court orders
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • To prevent fraud or illegal activity

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place including:

  • Adequacy decisions by the European Commission
  • Standard contractual clauses (SCCs)
  • Binding corporate rules
  • Compliance with applicable privacy laws

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Account Information: Until account deletion or 2 years of inactivity
  • Transaction Data: 7 years (for tax and legal purposes)
  • Support Communications: 3 years
  • Analytics Data: 26 months (anonymized after)

Your Privacy Rights

General Rights (Applicable to All Users)

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service

GDPR Rights (EU/UK Users)

  • Restrict Processing: Limit processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Object to Processing: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Complain to Supervisory Authority: Contact data protection authorities

CCPA Rights (California Users)

  • Right to Know: What personal information is collected and used
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of sale or sharing of personal information
  • Non-Discrimination: No discrimination for exercising privacy rights

PIPEDA Rights (Canadian Users)

  • Access and Correction: Access and correct personal information
  • Collection Limitation: Limit collection to necessary purposes
  • Use, Disclosure, and Retention: Clear policies on data handling
  • Safeguards: Appropriate security protections

Australian Privacy Act Rights

  • Access and Correction: Access and correct personal information
  • Use Limitation: Use only for intended purposes
  • Data Quality: Ensure accuracy and relevance
  • Security: Reasonable security safeguards

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures including:

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Access Controls: Restricted access to personal information
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and penetration testing
  • Employee Training: Privacy and security awareness programs
  • Incident Response: Procedures for data breach notification

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Policy
