Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

Effective date: February 23, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, which can make it easier to return to sites and make them more useful to you.

How We Use Cookies

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services.

  • Authentication: Remember your login status
  • Security: Prevent fraud and protect your data
  • Shopping Cart: Maintain items in your ticket cart
  • Preferences: Remember your display and language preferences

Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us to know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site.

  • Google Analytics: Website usage and performance analysis
  • Hotjar: User behavior and heat mapping (with consent)
  • Custom Analytics: Internal performance metrics

Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant adverts on other sites.

  • Google Ads: Personalized advertising
  • Facebook Pixel: Social media advertising and retargeting
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag: Professional audience targeting
  • Affiliate Tracking: Partner program performance tracking

Personalization Cookies

These cookies enable the website to remember choices you make (such as your user name, language, or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features.

  • User Preferences: Favorite teams, events, and seating preferences
  • Location Services: Localized content and event recommendations
  • Customization: Personalized dashboard and recommendations

Cookie Categories and Consent

We categorize cookies based on their purpose and require your consent for non-essential categories:

Category Purpose Consent Required Retention Period
Essential Website functionality, security, authentication No Session to 1 year
Analytics Performance analysis, user behavior insights Yes 26 months
Marketing Personalized advertising, retargeting Yes 13 months
Personalization Customized content, preferences Yes 12 months

Third-Party Cookies

We use various third-party services that may set their own cookies:

Analytics Services

  • Google Analytics: Website traffic and user behavior analysis
  • Google Tag Manager: Tag management and deployment

Advertising Networks

  • Google Ads: Search and display advertising
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Social media advertising
  • LinkedIn: Professional audience targeting

Payment Processors

  • Stripe: Secure payment processing
  • PayPal: Alternative payment method

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner where you can:

  • Accept All: Enable all cookie categories
  • Reject All: Only enable essential cookies
  • Customize: Choose specific categories to enable

Changing Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by:

  • Clicking the cookie icon in the website footer
  • Visiting our Cookie Policy page
  • Adjusting browser settings to block cookies
  • Using browser extensions to manage cookies

Browser-Specific Instructions

Chrome

  1. Click the three dots menu (⋮)
  2. Select "Settings" > "Privacy and security"
  3. Click "Cookies and other site data"
  4. Choose your preferred settings

Firefox

  1. Click the menu button (☰)
  2. Select "Options" > "Privacy & Security"
  3. Go to "Cookies and Site Data" section
  4. Manage your cookie preferences

Safari

  1. Click "Safari" > "Preferences"
  2. Select "Privacy" tab
  3. Click "Manage Website Data"
  4. Control cookie settings for individual sites

Edge

  1. Click the three dots menu (⋮)
  2. Select "Settings" > "Privacy, search, and services"
  3. Go to "Cookies and site permissions"
  4. Manage your cookie preferences

Regional Compliance

GDPR (European Union & United Kingdom)

  • Explicit consent required for non-essential cookies
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Transparent information about cookie usage
  • Easy mechanism to manage preferences

CCPA (California, USA)

  • Opt-out mechanism for cookie sales
  • "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link
  • Clear disclosure of data practices
  • Non-discrimination for privacy choices

PIPEDA (Canada)

  • Meaningful consent for cookie collection
  • Purpose limitation to specified objectives
  • Adequate safeguards for cookie data
  • Transparency in cookie practices

Australian Privacy Act

  • Reasonable collection of cookie data
  • Clear notice of cookie practices
  • Appropriate security safeguards
  • Access to cookie information on request

Cookie Lifespan

Different types of cookies have different lifespans:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by our website directly
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by external services we use

Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly:

  • Authentication: May need to log in repeatedly
  • Shopping Cart: Items may not be saved between sessions
  • Personalization: Generic experience instead of customized content
  • Analytics: Less ability to improve our services
